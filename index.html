Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS-friendly resume?
An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) friendly resume is specifically formatted to be easily read by the automated software employers use to screen candidates. It avoids complex tables, graphics, and unreadable fonts that cause tracking systems to reject your application automatically. Our builder creates pure vector-text PDFs that parse perfectly every time.
Is ZenResume really free to use?
Yes, ZenResume is completely free. Unlike other platforms that ask for a credit card right when you hit the download button, we provide high-quality PDF downloads at no cost. We are supported by optional advertisements and affiliate links.
Is my personal data secure?
Absolutely. All resume processing and AI importing happens securely. We do not sell your personal resume data to third-party recruiters. You have full control over your data and can delete your account at any time.
Can I import my existing resume?
Yes! Using our Gemini AI Magic Import feature, you can upload any messy PDF resume or copy-paste a text document. Our AI will automatically extract your experience, education, and skills and place them perfectly into our templates.
Does ZenResume support US Letter paper size?
Yes, we support both A4 and US Letter sizes dynamically. If you are located in the United States, our system automatically selects US Letter format on startup. You can also manually toggle paper sizes in one click in the editor preview bar.
What industries do your templates support?
Our resume layouts support a wide variety of industries, including Software Engineering, Core Engineering (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical), Medical & Clinical, and Law & Legal. Each template is custom-styled to fit industry standards.

💡 ZenResume User Guide & Tips

Get the most out of your resume building process with these tips and features.

✨ 1. Gemini AI Magic Import (PDF/Text)

Click the Magic Import button to upload your existing resume (PDF) or paste raw text. The built-in Gemini AI parser will automatically extract your contact details, work history, projects, and skills to pre-populate all form fields in one click!

💾 2. Auto-Save & Local Backup Files

• Auto-Save: ZenResume saves your progress automatically on every key press in your browser's local cache.
• Backup (JSON): Click Backup to export a lightweight file of your resume data. Keep this file safe!
• Restore: Click Restore and upload your backup file to instantly load your resume details on any device.

🖨️ 3. How to Export a Perfect PDF

When you click Download PDF at the final step, the browser's native print preview window will pop up. For the cleanest layout:
• Set Destination to Save as PDF.
• Set Margins to Default or None (margins are handled natively by templates).
• Uncheck Headers and footers (this removes the browser's date/URL text from the page edges).

📈 4. ATS-Friendly Formatting Rules

• Use standard, readable fonts (like Inter or Roboto).
• Keep bullet points clear, concise, and focused on accomplishments.
• Avoid inserting custom images, emojis, or charts directly inside text fields, as ATS bots cannot crawl them.
