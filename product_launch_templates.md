# 🚀 Launch Templates for Product Platforms

Product platforms (like HackerNews and IndieHackers) hate marketing language. They want to hear from a passionate developer who built a tool to solve a real problem. Copy and paste these exact templates.

---

### 1. Hacker News (news.ycombinator.com)
*Go to Hacker News, click "Submit", and use this exact title and text.*

**Title:** Show HN: I built a 100% free, client-side ATS Resume Builder
**URL:** https://resume-builder-swart-sigma-93.vercel.app/

**Text (Optional):**
> Hey HN,
>
> I got incredibly frustrated applying to jobs recently. Every "free" resume builder on Google lets you spend 45 minutes building a resume, only to hit you with a $15/month paywall right when you try to download the PDF. On top of that, many of the "pretty" Canva templates they offer are totally unreadable by modern ATS parsers, leading to auto-rejections.
>
> So I built ZenResume to solve my own problem. It is a completely free, open-source-minded builder. 
> 
> * **No Paywalls:** You don't even need to make an account.
> * **100% Client-Side:** Your data never leaves your browser. Privacy first.
> * **ATS-Strict Format:** It forces a single-column, standard font architecture that guarantees ATS parsers can read every word.
> 
> I'd love for the community to try it out and give me some harsh feedback on the UX or the PDF generation (using html2pdf).
