3. ATS Formatting Rules for Tax Consultant

  • Single-Column Hierarchy: Keep headers standard (Work Experience, Skills, Education) so automated parsers read in chronological order.
  • Quantifiable Metrics: Include percentages, dollar values, or scale numbers in at least 70% of your experience bullets.
  • Pure Vector Export: Export your resume as a clean selectable-text PDF rather than a scanned image to ensure 100% keyword indexing.
