**Text:**
> Hey HN,

> I built ZenResume because I got incredibly frustrated with the "dark patterns" in the resume builder space. Every "free" resume builder on Google lets you spend 45 minutes building a resume, only to hit you with a $15/month paywall right when you click "Download PDF".
