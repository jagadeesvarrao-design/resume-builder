About ZenResume

Empowering professionals and students to craft recruiter-approved, ATS-compliant resumes with 100% privacy and zero paywalls.

1. Our Mission

ZenResume was founded with a singular purpose: to eliminate unfair financial paywalls in the job search process. The vast majority of online resume tools lure users with "free templates" only to lock the PDF download behind mandatory recurring subscriptions. We believe that career advancement tools should be universally accessible, lightning-fast, and completely transparent.

2. The ZenResume Core Pillars

  • 100% Free Forever: No surprise paywalls, no forced sign-ups, and no watermark fees.
  • Privacy-First Local Storage: Your personal employment records remain stored strictly inside your browser (IndexedDB and local cache) with complete JSON and .zenresume portable data ownership. We operate zero remote databases that harvest or store user profiles.
  • Enterprise ATS Compliance: Every layout is built using single-column semantic HTML rendered to selectable vector PDFs tested across Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever.
  • Data-Backed Editorial Authority: Our career guides and role benchmarks are continuously updated to reflect real-world hiring standards and recruiter feedback.

ZenResume is developed, maintained, and published by Aneevarp Solutions, an official technology enterprise registered under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India (Registration No: UDYAM-AP-10-0144446), headquartered in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.

4. The Aneevarp Solutions Career Ecosystem

Aneevarp Solutions engineers privacy-first, local-first career acceleration platforms designed to eliminate unfair financial barriers for global job seekers:

  • ZenResume (zenresume.online): A recruiter-approved ATS resume builder with 63 role templates, granular layout customizers, 1-click single page auto-fit, and instant vector PDF export.
  • ZenScout AI (ZenScout AI Portal): An autonomous multi-agent job hunting platform that monitors live Google Jobs, scores resume compatibility, crafts personalized cover letters, and conducts interactive AI mock interviews.
