About ZenResume

Empowering professionals and students to craft recruiter-approved, ATS-compliant resumes with 100% privacy and zero paywalls.

1. Our Mission

ZenResume was founded with a singular purpose: to eliminate unfair financial paywalls in the job search process. The vast majority of online resume tools lure users with "free templates" only to lock the PDF download behind mandatory recurring subscriptions. We believe that career advancement tools should be universally accessible, lightning-fast, and completely transparent.

2. The ZenResume Core Pillars

  • 100% Free Forever: No surprise paywalls, no forced sign-ups, and no watermark fees.
  • Privacy-First Local Storage: Your personal employment records remain stored strictly inside your own browser (localStorage). We do not harvest or sell your data.
  • Enterprise ATS Compliance: Every layout is built using semantic HTML rendered to selectable vector PDFs tested across Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever.
  • Data-Backed Editorial Authority: Our career guides and role benchmarks are continuously updated to reflect 2026 corporate hiring data and real recruiter feedback.

3. Publisher & Legal Entity

ZenResume is developed, maintained, and published by Aneevarp Solutions, an official technology and software publishing enterprise registered under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India (Registration No: UDYAM-AP-10-0144446), headquartered in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.

4. Editorial & Technical Leadership

ZenResume is maintained by a dedicated team of software engineers, career strategists, and talent acquisition professionals. We combine deep technical expertise in web performance and PDF rendering engines with hands-on recruiting experience to give job seekers an undeniable competitive advantage.
