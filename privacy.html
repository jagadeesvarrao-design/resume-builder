ZenResume Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026 • Effective Date: Immediate

At ZenResume, accessible from https://www.zenresume.online, our primary priority is safeguarding the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy details the types of information collected, how it is used, and mandatory disclosures regarding third-party advertising partners like Google AdSense.

1. 100% Client-Side Local Storage & Data Security

Unlike legacy resume platforms that transmit sensitive personal information to third-party databases, ZenResume operates on a strict Local-First client-side architecture. All resume text, contact details, work history, and education entered into our editor are stored exclusively in your browser's private local cache (localStorage). We do not operate server-side databases that harvest or store your resume content.

2. Cookies & Advertising Disclosures (Google AdSense Requirements)

In accordance with Google AdSense program policies, we disclose the following cookie and advertising practices:

  • Third-Party Vendors: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to our visitors based on their visit to ZenResume and other sites on the World Wide Web.
  • User Opt-Out Rights: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Analytics & Speed Insights

We utilize lightweight web analytics (such as Vercel Web Analytics and Google Analytics) to monitor aggregate traffic trends, page load speeds, and geographic regions. These tools collect non-personally identifiable telemetry to optimize site performance.

4. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Request Data Erasure: Because your data is stored locally in your browser, clearing your browser's cache/localStorage immediately and permanently deletes all resume records.
  • Opt-Out of Data Sale: We do not sell, lease, or monetize your personal resume data.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Team

For questions regarding this policy or data privacy practices, contact our support team at support@zenresume.online.
