The 3 Silent Failures of Canva Resume Templates

When you export a PDF from Canva, text elements are saved as arbitrary visual coordinates rather than an ordered semantic hierarchy. When an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) scans the document:

1. Horizontal Reading Errors: The parser reads across columns horizontally, merging your left-hand contact details directly into your right-hand work experience.
2. Missing Section Headers: Canva's decorative fonts and unstandardized headings prevent the parser from detecting where "Education" or "Work Experience" begins.
3. Skill Meter Blindness: Visual progress bars ("★★★★☆" or "90% proficiency") are treated as empty shapes, losing critical keywords from your candidate profile.
