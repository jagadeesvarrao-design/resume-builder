Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a 2-page resume on ZenResume for free?
Yes! ZenResume allows you to create 1-page, 2-page, or multi-page resumes without any limits or paywalls. Experienced engineers and executives can showcase their full career trajectory for free.
Why do skill rating bars on Novoresume fail ATS scans?
Skill rating bars (e.g., "Python: 4/5 dots") are visual graphics. ATS algorithms cannot read graphical progress meters and recruiters view self-assigned percentage ratings as subjective fluff. ZenResume lists skills as clean, searchable semantic tags.
How do I export my resume from ZenResume?
Click the "Download PDF" button in the builder top bar. ZenResume generates an instant, clean, high-resolution vector PDF directly from your browser with zero watermarks.
