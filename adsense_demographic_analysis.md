## 1. How Google AdSense Pays You

Google AdSense pays you based on two main metrics: **Impressions (CPM)** and **Clicks (CPC)**.

### A. Cost Per Mille (CPM) - "Pay per View"
Every time an ad loads on your screen, you get paid a tiny fraction of a cent. Google calculates this in groups of 1,000 (Mille). 
- If your CPM is **$0.20**, you make 20 cents for every 1,000 times an ad is loaded.
- Because ZenResume has **5 to 6 ads per page**, 1 single user visiting 1 page generates 5 to 6 ad impressions.

### B. Cost Per Click (CPC) - "Pay per Action"
This is where the real money is made. If a user actually clicks on an ad, Google pays you a much higher rate.
- In India, a click might pay **$0.02 to $0.10**.
- In the US/UK, a click might pay **$0.50 to $2.00**.
- Usually, about 1% to 2% of users will click an ad (this is called your Click-Through Rate, or CTR).

### C. Page RPM (Revenue Per Mille)
To make it easy to understand, Google combines your CPM and CPC into one metric called **Page RPM**. 
*Page RPM = How much money you make for every 1,000 page views on your website.*
